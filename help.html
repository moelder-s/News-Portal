Getting Started & Basics

What is News In Green and how does it work?

Learn about our student-run news portal covering campus life, events, academics, and sports.

Do I need an account to read news?

No, all news articles are public! However, creating an account lets you save bookmarks and submit articles.

How do I switch between dark and light modes?

Click the theme toggle button on the top right header to switch display modes at any time.

How do bookmarks work?

Click the bookmark icon on any news card to save it to your personal Bookmarks page for quick reading later.

Submitting & Article Publishing

How do I submit an article?

Log in to your student account, navigate to the Account section in the footer, and select "Submit an article".

Am I eligible to become a student contributor?

Any registered student or faculty member can write and submit drafts for review by our editor team.

How long does article approval take?

Submitted articles are usually reviewed by our editorial team within 24 to 48 hours before going live.

Can I edit my article after publishing?

You can request minor updates or corrections by contacting the editors at newsroom@newsingreen.edu.

Account & Newsletter Management

How do I subscribe to the weekly newsletter?

Enter your email address in the subscription box on the home page or turn on email notifications in settings.

How can I update my profile details?

Log in and open your profile settings to change your name, avatar, or registered email address.

Technical & Troubleshooting

What should I do if I forgot my password?

Click "Log in" on the top header, select "Forgot Password", and follow the instructions sent to your email.

Who do I contact for website bugs or issues?

Reach out to our developer team directly at newsroom@newsingreen.edu with details of the issue.
