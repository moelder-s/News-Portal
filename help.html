Getting Started & Basics

What is News In Green and how does it work?

Learn about our student-run news portal covering campus life, events, academics, and sports.

Do I need an account to read news?

No, all news articles are public! However, creating an account lets you save bookmarks and submit articles.

How do I switch between dark and light modes?

Click the theme toggle button on the top right header to switch display modes at any time.

How do bookmarks work?

Click the bookmark icon on any news card to save it to your personal Bookmarks page for quick reading later.
